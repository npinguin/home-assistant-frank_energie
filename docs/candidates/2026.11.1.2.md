# Frank Energie 2026.11.1.2 — BE current-day gas recovery / finance evidence HACS TEST

**Status:** controlled pre-release candidate. Not stable, not invoice-accounting accepted.
**Source:** single consolidated Draft PR #18 on `fix/be-price-provenance-truth`.
**HA baseline:** last installed Frank Energie `2026.11.1.1` on HA Core `2026.10.4`
in Belgium; no real Home Assistant run of this new code has yet been observed.

## Why this candidate exists

The last installed diagnostics at 2026-11-01 00:21 local show an authenticated
BE `MODEL` variable contract with both electricity and gas selected, 24
hourly customer electricity prices but only 6 hourly gas prices for the day.
This is **incomplete current-day source coverage**, not missing subscription.
The requested public market resolution `PT15M` differs from the effective
hourly customer price stream. Price coordinator and statistics coordinator
reported successful updates, but that does **not** establish individual HA
sensor states, fresh meter readings, correct invoice costs, or Recorder writes.

## Corrections since the previous immutable test build

- Current-day price cache is now checked for continuous local-day coverage
  of every selected electricity/gas segment (including 23/25-hour DST days).
  Partial current-day gas prices are retried at the normal coordinator cadence
  instead of being frozen until next midnight.
- A shorter/empty replacement API segment cannot erase more complete
  current-day prices already held in memory. Later full source coverage
  replaces the incomplete segment without fabricating prices.
- MODEL customer hourly resolution is accepted even when a public market
  request selects `PT15M`, but only for the explicitly selected MODEL site.
  A different delivery site's contract cannot waive cache validation.
- Old today/tomorrow event, config-entry, device/entity identity and
  Recorder contract remain unchanged.
- Branch-only diagnostics now report HA entity availability/last-report
  summary, provider data freshness, gas pipeline status and sanitized finance
  quality without exporting customer amounts or identifiers.
- Opt-in monthly finance uses raw `MonthInsights` field evidence before
  pinned client null-to-zero coercion. Current/previous month sensors remain
  disabled by default; no invoice totals or fictitious capacity tariffs.

2. Capture redacted HA diagnostics after first startup and later normal price

4. Verify anonymous/public versus authenticated/customer fallback when a
   selected stream is partial. A public spot price is **not** a Belgian

5. Only after explicit opt-in for a controlled test, inspect raw
   `MonthInsights` month-boundary source evidence for both current and prior
   months: missing/null versus true zero, gas and electricity usage/costs,
   feed-in sign, meter cutoff equality and source reconciliation.
6. Compare the **consumption-month settlement components** of real Belgian
   invoices with the corresponding provider period, not the invoice's cash
   payment total, which can include advances for another month.
7. Verify HACS install/update/download/rollback, option reload/restart,
   selected site identity, no duplicated devices, no Recorder history loss
   and no identifiers or monetary amounts in exported diagnostics.
